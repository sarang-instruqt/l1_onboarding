 # Welcome to L1 Support

Welcome to the Instruqt L1 Support onboarding lab! 🎉

This onboarding guide will help you understand the tools, workflows, responsibilities, and processes required to operate effectively as an L1 Support team member at Instruqt.

During this lab, you will learn about:

1. Account setup and internal tools
2. Product knowledge and platform basics
3. Support workflows and escalation procedures
4. Ticket handling best practices
5. Performance expectations
6. Team collaboration and communication

## Objectives

By the end of this onboarding lab, you should be able to:

- Navigate the core support tools
- Understand the L1 support workflow
- Handle and categorize support tickets
- Escalate issues appropriately
- Collaborate effectively with internal teams
- Follow support expectations and best practices

> [!NOTE]
> If you have questions during onboarding, reach out to your mentor, team lead, or the #xebia-india-ops Slack channel.
