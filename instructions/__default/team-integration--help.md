 # Team Integration & Help

Support operations are highly collaborative, and successful onboarding includes becoming familiar with team communication practices, support routines, and internal collaboration workflows.

This section covers team integration expectations and available support resources.

---

## Daily Stand-Up Meetings

Attend daily stand-up meetings regularly.

These meetings help teams:

- Share operational updates
- Discuss ongoing issues
- Review blockers
- Coordinate priorities
- Communicate incident information

> [!TIP]
> Keep updates concise and highlight any blockers or urgent support concerns.

---

## Weekly Support Team Sync

Participate in recurring support team sync meetings.

Topics may include:

- Operational updates
- Incident reviews
- Process improvements
- Escalation learnings
- Customer feedback
- Platform updates

These meetings help align support operations across the team.

---

## Schedule 1:1 Meetings

New team members are encouraged to schedule 1:1 sessions with:

- Mentors
- Team leads
- Fellow support engineers
- Cross-functional teams

1:1 meetings are useful for:

- Asking questions
- Receiving feedback
- Understanding workflows
- Building team relationships

---

## Customer Success Meetings

Join customer success meetings as an observer when possible.

This helps provide additional visibility into:

- Customer expectations
- Platform adoption challenges
- Common customer concerns
- Communication best practices

---

## Asking for Help

If you encounter questions or blockers during onboarding:

- Reach out to your mentor
- Ask in the support team Slack channel
- Contact your team lead
- Refer to the internal knowledge base

Useful channels and resources:

- `#support-team`
- Internal documentation
- Troubleshooting guides
- Operational runbooks

> [!NOTE]
> Asking questions early helps improve onboarding speed and operational confidence.

---

## Collaboration Culture

Successful support operations rely on:

- Team collaboration
- Clear communication
- Knowledge sharing
- Continuous improvement
- Mutual support

Everyone is encouraged to contribute ideas, feedback, and learnings.
