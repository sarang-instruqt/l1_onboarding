 # Ticket Handling

Effective ticket handling is one of the most important responsibilities of the L1 Support team.

This section covers onboarding expectations, ticket review processes, customer communication, and troubleshooting practices.

---

## Shadow Experienced Team Members

During onboarding, spend time observing experienced L1 Support engineers handling tickets and customer interactions.

Focus on understanding:

- Ticket triage workflow
- Customer communication style
- Troubleshooting approaches
- Escalation decisions
- Internal collaboration practices

> [!TIP]
> Take notes during shadowing sessions to build your own troubleshooting reference guide.

---

## Initial Ticket Handling

New team members should begin handling tickets with mentor guidance and review.

### Onboarding Expectations

- Handle at least 5 tickets with mentor review
- Ask questions during troubleshooting
- Request feedback on responses and ticket updates
- Learn escalation expectations and workflows

The goal is to gradually build confidence and familiarity with the support process.

---

## Independent Ticket Handling

After completing the initial onboarding phase, new hires should begin managing tickets independently.

### Expected Milestone

- Successfully handle at least 20 tickets independently

This helps reinforce:

- Troubleshooting confidence
- Prioritization skills
- Communication consistency
- Escalation judgment

---

## Ticket Response Best Practices

When responding to customer tickets:

- Acknowledge the issue promptly
- Be professional and empathetic
- Provide clear next steps
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon
- Keep customers updated regularly
- Document troubleshooting actions clearly

---

## Ticket Documentation

Accurate documentation is essential for collaboration and escalations.

Include details such as:

- Issue summary
- Troubleshooting steps performed
- Customer impact
- Relevant screenshots or logs
- Escalation notes
- Resolution details

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Well-documented tickets reduce investigation time and improve team collaboration.

---

## Ticket Prioritization

Prioritize tickets based on:

- Severity
- Customer impact
- Business urgency
- Platform-wide impact
- Time sensitivity

Examples:

| Priority | Example |
|---|---|
| Critical | Production outage affecting multiple users |
| High | Platform issue or bug |
| Medium | Feature partially unavailable |
| Low | Informational request or minor issue |

---

## Working with Unthread

Unthread is the primary support ticketing platform used for customer communication and ticket management.

Typical workflows include:

- Reviewing new tickets
- Assigning ownership
- Updating customer responses
- Adding internal notes
- Escalating issues when required

Useful resource:

- [Unthread Platform](https://instruqt.unthread.io/dashboard/home)

---

## Asking Questions & Feedback

Support onboarding is collaborative.

You are encouraged to:

- Ask questions frequently
- Request ticket reviews
- Discuss troubleshooting approaches
- Share learnings with the team

Mentor feedback helps improve both technical troubleshooting and customer communication skills.

---

## Common Goals During Onboarding

By the end of onboarding, you should be comfortable with:

- Handling standard support tickets
- Identifying escalation scenarios
- Communicating effectively with customers
- Navigating support tools
- Collaborating with internal teams

> [!NOTE]
> Continuous learning and consistent communication are key to becoming successful.
