 # Support Processes & Procedures

Understanding the support workflow and escalation procedures is essential for effective L1 Support operations.

This section covers incident handling, escalation expectations, ticket prioritization, and communication practices.

---

## L1 Support Workflow

As an L1 Support team member, your responsibilities typically include:

- Monitoring tickets
- Performing initial troubleshooting
- Categorizing and prioritizing issues
- Responding to customers
- Escalating issues when required
- Documenting findings and updates

The goal of L1 Support is to provide timely responses and resolve common issues efficiently.

---

## Ticket Triage & Categorization

Proper ticket triage helps ensure issues are routed and prioritized correctly.

When reviewing tickets:

- Identify the issue category
- Determine severity and impact
- Gather relevant troubleshooting details
- Check for existing incidents or known issues
- Assign appropriate priority levels
- Add tag

Common ticket categories may include:

- Platform Issues
- Sandbox Failures
- Authentication Problems
- Access Requests
- Lab/Track Issues
- Integration Problems
- Performance Issues

> [!TIP]
> Clear and accurate ticket categorization improves escalation efficiency and reduces resolution time.

## Incident Severity Awareness

Understand the impact level of incidents before escalating.

### Example Severity Levels

| Severity | Description | 
|---|---|
| P1 | Critical platform outage or major customer impact (Critical) |
| P2 | Significant functionality affected in clude as High (Hight Priority)  |
| P3 | Minor issues or degraded experience (Medium) |
| P4 | Informational requests or low-impact issues(Low)|

---

## P1 Incident Response Guide

P1 incidents require immediate attention and escalation.

During a P1 incident:

- Acknowledge the issue quickly
- Gather relevant information
- Notify the appropriate teams
- Escalate according to the incident process
- Provide updates in the designated communication channels

Useful resource:

- [P1 Incident Response Guide](https://uptime.betterstack.com/report/Avt4rr7V1dGzni5JEzqeBJqs3)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never delay escalation for confirmed high-severity production incidents.

## Escalation Procedures

Escalate issues to L2 or Engineering when:

- The issue requires deeper technical investigation
- Platform infrastructure appears affected
- Customer impact is widespread
- Logs or internal tools indicate backend issues
- You are blocked after initial troubleshooting

When escalating, include:

- Ticket reference
- Customer impact summary
- Steps already performed
- Relevant screenshots or logs
- Timeline of the issue

> [!NOTE]
> High-quality escalation notes help reduce resolution time and improve collaboration with L2 and Engineering teams.

## Standard Customer Responses

Use professional and consistent communication when responding to customers.

Good support responses should:

- Acknowledge the issue clearly
- Set expectations appropriately
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon
- Provide actionable next steps
- Maintain a professional tone
