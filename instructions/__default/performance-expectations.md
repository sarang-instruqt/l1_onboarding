 # Performance Expectations

As an L1 Support team member, maintaining strong operational performance and customer communication standards is essential.

This section outlines the key expectations, goals, and best practices for daily support operations.

---

## Ticket Ownership

L1 Support engineers are expected to actively manage and follow up on assigned tickets.

This includes:

- Reviewing ticket updates regularly
- Following up with customers when needed
- Providing status updates
- Ensuring proper documentation
- Escalating issues appropriately

> [!TIP]
> Maintaining clear ownership improves customer trust and reduces delays in resolution.

---

## Ticket Acknowledgement SLA

Support tickets should be acknowledged promptly.

### Target Expectation

- Acknowledge incoming tickets within **15 minutes**

A quick acknowledgement helps reassure customers that their issue is being reviewed.

---

## Ticket Categorization & Prioritization

Proper ticket management includes:

- Categorizing issues accurately
- Assigning appropriate priority levels
- Identifying urgent incidents quickly
- Routing tickets correctly when escalation is needed

Good prioritization helps the team respond efficiently during high-volume periods.

---

## Customer Satisfaction

Customer experience is a critical part of support operations.

### Target Goal

- Maintain at least a **4 out of 5 customer satisfaction score**

Strong customer communication includes:

- Professional responses
- Clear troubleshooting steps
- Timely updates
- Empathy and accountability

---

## Troubleshooting Expectations

L1 Support should gather sufficient troubleshooting information before escalating issues.

Examples include:

- Error messages
- Screenshots
- Logs
- Reproduction steps
- Environment details
- Customer impact summary

> [!IMPORTANT]
> High-quality troubleshooting details improve escalation efficiency and reduce investigation time for L2 and Engineering teams.

---

## Knowledge Base Contributions

Support engineers are encouraged to contribute to internal documentation and knowledge sharing.

Examples include:

- Common troubleshooting steps
- Known issue documentation
- Customer issue summaries
- Internal process improvements

Useful resource:

- [Internal Knowledge Base](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jSug0aaecFkInkURoLHheIi5G3pEzt3p)

---

## Reducing Dependency on L2

One of the goals during onboarding is to gradually improve independent troubleshooting skills.

This includes:

- Understanding common issues
- Identifying root causes
- Performing initial investigations
- Escalating only when necessary

When escalation is required, provide complete and accurate troubleshooting information.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Support operations evolve continuously.

Team members are encouraged to:

- Learn from incidents
- Review past tickets
- Improve troubleshooting workflows
- Share feedback with the team
- Stay updated on platform changes

---

## Operational Excellence

Successful L1 Support engineers demonstrate:

- Strong communication skills
- Attention to detail
- Ownership mindset
- Collaborative teamwork
- Customer-focused thinking
- Consistent follow-through

> [!NOTE]
> Consistency, communication, and proactive troubleshooting are key factors for long-term success in L1 Support.
