 # Product Knowledge

To provide effective customer support, it is important to understand the core concepts, workflows, and capabilities of the Instruqt platform.

This section introduces the foundational product knowledge required for L1 Support operations.

---

## Instruqt Platform Overview

Instruqt enables organizations to create interactive hands-on learning experiences using tracks, challenges, and sandbox environments.

The platform helps customers deliver:

- Technical workshops
- Product demonstrations
- Self-paced training
- Certifications
- Interactive onboarding experiences

---

## Core Platform Concepts

### Tracks

Tracks are collections of learning modules or challenges that guide users through a structured experience.

Tracks may include:

- Instructions
- Activities
- Quizzes
- Sandbox environments
- Validation checks

---

### Challenges

Challenges are individual learning units within a track.

A challenge can contain:

- Step-by-step instructions
- Activities
- Validation scripts
- Embedded media
- Interactive tasks

---

### Sandboxes

Sandboxes provide temporary environments for users to interact with technologies and complete hands-on tasks.

Common sandbox types include:

- Linux containers
- Kubernetes clusters
- Cloud environments
- Development workspaces

> [!NOTE]
> Not all tracks require sandbox environments. Some onboarding or documentation-focused tracks may only contain instructional content.

---

## User Roles & Permissions

Understand the different user roles available within the platform.

Examples may include:

- Owner
- Content Creator
- Member
- Instructor
- Instructor (No invite creation )

Permissions determine what actions users can perform within the platform.

Examples include:

- Salesforce

Understanding integrations helps troubleshoot customer setup and configuration issues.

## Support Perspective

As an L1 Support Engineer, your goal is to:

- Understand common customer issues
- Identify platform-related problems
- Gather troubleshooting information
- Escalate issues effectively when needed
- Provide accurate and timely responses

A strong understanding of the product improves troubleshooting efficiency and customer satisfaction.
